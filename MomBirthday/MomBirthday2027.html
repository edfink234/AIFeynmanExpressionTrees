Happy
Birthday, Mom

59 beautiful years of you

I made this little corner of the internet for the person who has given me more care, honesty, and love than I have always known how to recognize.

Mom, you are one of the most caring and kind people I know. You have done so much for me—often quietly, often before I even understood what you were doing.

You found PhD programs for me. You found the SMART scholarship that changed what was possible for me. You researched, noticed, questioned, and kept looking until you found something useful. That is who you are: a natural researcher with an extraordinary eye for what matters.

You see things clearly. You say what you truly think. You do not hide behind a mask just to make other people comfortable—and your honesty, even when it is hard for me to hear, is one of the things I value most about you.

I also want to say something plainly: I know I have caused you pain, misunderstood you, and taken your care for granted in ways I never meant to. I am deeply sorry. You deserved to be seen, listened to, and cared for—not only relied upon.

I am trying to understand you better: your sensitive, perceptive nervous system; how much your body asks you to carry; the pain and exhaustion that do not make you any less strong. You should not have to prove how much you feel or how much you do.
